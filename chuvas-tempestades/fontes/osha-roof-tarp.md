Scope: roofing work and installing plastic sheeting or tarps on wind-damaged roofs; ladders/scaffolds, power tools, work near power lines, steep, slippery or deteriorating surfaces.

Falls (primary hazard):
- Limit access; controlled access zones.
- Low-slope roofs (4:12 or less) with unprotected edges 6 ft+ above lower level: guardrails, safety nets or personal fall arrest; or warning line combined with another system. Safety monitoring alone only on roofs 50 ft or less wide.
- Steep roofs (over 4:12), edges 6 ft+: guardrails with toeboards, safety nets or personal fall arrest.
- Cover/guard holes as soon as created; covers support twice the expected load and be marked "Danger - Roof Opening"/"Hole".
- Mark areas with inadequate structural support "Danger - No Step".
- Keep occupants out of areas under overhead roof work.
- Fall arrest anchors rated 5,000 lb; account for worker height, shock absorber stretch, lanyard length.

Ladders/scaffolds: inspect; don't exceed load rating; straight/extension ladders at about 75 degrees, extending 3 ft above landing, secured; non-conductive ladders near power lines; scaffolds plumb, braced, fully planked, guardrails at 10 ft or higher.

Electrical: assume lines are energized until proven otherwise (can re-energize without warning); stay away from downed conductors and anything they may energize (fences, pipes, cables); keep at least 10 ft from overhead lines; GFCI or double-insulated tools; avoid wet areas.

Tools/noise: nailer safety devices working; no defective tools; hearing protection above 90 dBA.

Tarping and material handling: secure tarp rolls before lifting; more than one person or lifting equipment; take care unrolling tarps on the roof; choose non-reflective, textured tarps; keep surfaces dry; let damp tarps dry before walking on them; flat-soled slip-resistant footwear.

Generators: never connect to building wiring without transfer switch installed by qualified electrician; never indoors/enclosed spaces (CO); keep dry and 3–4 ft clear on all sides; shut down before refueling.

PPE: hard hat, eye protection with side shields, gloves, protective footwear, respiratory protection as needed, fall protection, hearing protection.
