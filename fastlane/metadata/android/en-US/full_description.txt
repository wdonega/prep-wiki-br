An offline library for preparedness, self-reliance and health, in Brazilian Portuguese.

When the network goes down (a flood, a blackout, out in the countryside or on the road) what you need to know has to be on your phone. Pick what to download from a curated list of 81 collections; once downloaded, everything works without internet.

WHAT YOU CAN DOWNLOAD
• Portuguese Wikipedia: complete (with or without images) or only the 50,000 best articles, starting at 153 MB
• Wikipedia by topic: Medicine, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Biology, History, Geography, Computing
• Wikis made by this project: Sisteminha Embrapa (backyard food production), Plantas Medicinais (85 plants from the Brazilian health agency's herbal formulary) and a heavy rain and storm guide
• Study material, repairs (iFixit), books (Wikisource, Project Gutenberg), dictionary, travel guides and maps of Brazil
• Preparedness material in English: survival, field medicine, water treatment, canning, gardening, energy, repairs and amateur radio

HOW IT WORKS
• Search by meaning: ask in your own words and the app searches every installed collection, on the device
• On-device translation: pages and PDFs in English can be read in Portuguese, without internet
• Reader for pages, PDFs and videos, with light or dark theme in three colors
• Downloads keep going with the screen off, showing the size of each collection and the free space

PRIVACY
No account, no ads and no tracking. The app only uses the internet to download the collections you choose. What you search for and read never leaves the device.

NOTICE
The content is informational and comes from open sources such as Wikipedia. It does not replace medical advice or emergency care.

Collections are stored in the app's folder and are deleted if the app is uninstalled. They are ZIM files, the same ones Kiwix uses, read with libkiwix. Open source (GPLv3): https://github.com/wdonega/prep-wiki-br
